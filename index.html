About me.

A discipline, a few different cities, and a growing curiosity about how we represent the world.

From planning cities to exploring the data behind them.

My background is in spatial planning, but I've gradually become interested in the tools and methods that let us examine space from different perspectives — from geographic information systems to building information modelling and 3D reconstruction.

I enjoy moving between disciplines, asking how different kinds of spatial information fit together, and exploring how digital representations can support a better understanding of the built environment.

Research internship Belgium

Exploring 3D reconstruction

KU Leuven · Geomatics Research Group

During my research internship, I explored and compared different approaches to 3D reconstruction, working with COLMAP, GLOMAP, GlueMap and VGGT. My work focused on comparing workflows, examining reconstruction accuracy and inspecting the resulting point clouds.

This experience helped me explore how different reconstruction methods represent spatial environments, and how their outputs can be examined and compared.

Bachelor's degree Poland

Connecting spatial planning and digital models

Warsaw University of Technology

My bachelor's studies in Spatial Planning gave me a foundation in urban development, spatial analysis and the planning of the built environment. Alongside this, I became increasingly interested in digital tools for representing and analysing cities.

My bachelor's thesis examined City Information Modeling in Spatial Planning through a systematic literature review. It brought together my interest in planning with questions about how digital city models can contribute to spatial planning practice and research.
