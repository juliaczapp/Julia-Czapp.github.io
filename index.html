About me.

My background is in spatial planning. My curiosity has taken me into geospatial technologies, 3D data and the digital representation of cities.

Interdisciplinary work Connecting planning and technology

I studied Spatial Planning at Warsaw University of Technology, where I developed an interest in the relationship between cities, spatial information and the digital tools used to understand them.

That interest gradually led me beyond conventional planning workflows. Through GIS, BIM and City Information Modeling, I became increasingly curious about how spatial information is collected, structured, represented and analysed.

During my research internship at KU Leuven, I explored different 3D reconstruction workflows and compared their approaches, accuracy and point-cloud outputs. This gave me another perspective on spatial data: not only how it is used, but how digital representations of the physical world are created.

I am interested in developing this combination of planning knowledge and geospatial methods further, particularly through advanced study and research. I see this portfolio as a record of that process — a place to document projects, test ideas and make connections between disciplines.

Research internship Belgium

Exploring 3D reconstruction workflows

KU Leuven · Geomatics Research Group

During my research internship, I explored and compared different 3D reconstruction approaches, working with COLMAP, GLOMAP, GlueMap and VGGT. I examined differences between workflows, reconstruction accuracy and resulting point-cloud outputs.

The experience helped me understand how alternative methods can produce different representations of a scene, and how these outputs can be inspected and compared. It also strengthened my interest in geospatial research beyond conventional planning tools.

Bachelor's degree Poland

Spatial planning and City Information Modeling

Warsaw University of Technology

My studies in Spatial Planning gave me a foundation in urban development, spatial analysis and the planning of the built environment. Alongside this, I developed an interest in digital approaches to representing cities and organising spatial information.

My bachelor's thesis was a systematic literature review of City Information Modeling in Spatial Planning. It brought together my planning background and my interest in how digital city models can contribute to spatial planning practice and research.
